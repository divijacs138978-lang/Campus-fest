## 📌 Problem Statement

Managing campus events, cultural festivals, and technical symposiums often suffers from fragmented communication, scattered registration channels, and poor participant engagement. Traditional methods relying on manual sign-up forms or disconnected social media posts lead to:

1. **Information Asymmetry:** Students frequently miss out on key event details, schedules, updates, or venue changes.
2. **Registration Bottlenecks:** Manual tracking of attendee registrations and team participation causes administrative overhead and data errors.
3. **Low Engagement:** Lack of a centralized platform prevents students from discovering, filtering, and interacting with events across different departments and clubs effectively.

**Campus Fest** solves this by providing a unified, real-time digital platform designed to streamline event discovery, simplify student registrations, and modernize campus event coordination.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Event Catalog & Discovery:** Browse upcoming technical, cultural, and sports events with clear categories and schedules.
* **Instant Registration:** Quick registration flow for participants and teams.
* **Real-time Event Information:** Access detailed agendas, rules, prize pools, and venue locations in one dashboard.
* **AI-Powered Assistance:** Integrated Google Gemini API for smart query handling and event insights.
